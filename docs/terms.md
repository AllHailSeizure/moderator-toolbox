## 1. Acceptance

## 2. What the app does

The app automates moderation rules that a subreddit's moderators configure (posting quotas, post length limits, comment depth caps, self-reply handling, nonresponder warnings, flair awards, and ignoring reports on bot comments) and gives moderators menu tools (Chain Mop, Saved Responses, Flood Quota Checker). It can remove, lock, report and comment on content in the installing subreddit, acting with the permissions the moderators grant it.

## 3. Moderator responsibility

Moderators choose which modules are on and what they do. Automated actions can be wrong, for example when Reddit data is late or incomplete. Moderators should review the app's actions and can approve or restore content at any time. The app is a tool for moderators, not a replacement for their judgment.
